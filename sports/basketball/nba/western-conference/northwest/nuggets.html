Team History

1967–1976: ABA Beginnings

The franchise began in 1967 as the Denver Rockets and competed in the American Basketball Association (ABA). The team developed its identity before eventually joining the NBA.

1976–1980: Joining the NBA

In 1976, the Denver Nuggets joined the NBA following the ABA–NBA merger. The team began competing against the league's established franchises.

1980s: The Alex English Era

Alex English became one of Denver's greatest stars during the 1980s. Alongside other talented players, he helped make the Nuggets known for their exciting, high-scoring basketball.

1990s: Defensive Greatness

Dikembe Mutombo became a defensive force in Denver. In the 1994 playoffs, the Nuggets made history by becoming the first eighth-seeded team to defeat a first-seeded team in a best-of-five opening round, eliminating the Seattle SuperSonics.

2000s: The Carmelo Anthony Era

Carmelo Anthony helped lead a new generation of Nuggets basketball after arriving in 2003. Denver regularly competed for playoff positions and reached the Western Conference Finals in 2009.

2020s: The First NBA Championship

Led by Nikola Jokić and Jamal Murray, the Nuggets won their first NBA championship in 2023. Denver defeated the Miami Heat in the NBA Finals, completing a historic season for the franchise.
